Meetings, transcribed.
Right on your Mac.

meet records your microphone and system audio, transcribes it on-device with whisper.cpp, and writes clean timestamped markdown. No cloud, no API keys, no subscription, no data ever leaving your machine.

Quick Start → All features

Why meet

The differentiators — what makes this a better choice for private, offline meeting notes.

  • 100% local & private. Audio is transcribed on your machine with whisper.cpp. Nothing is uploaded, ever — no account, no API keys, no third party in the loop.
  • Works offline. Once the model is downloaded, the whole pipeline runs with no network connection. Plan rides, flights, bad coffee-shop Wi-Fi.
  • No subscription, no per-minute billing. Free forever, MIT-licensed, open source. Run it as much as you want.
  • Metal GPU acceleration. whisper.cpp uses the Apple Silicon GPU by default — fast live transcription without a discrete card.
  • Dual-channel capture. Records you (mic) and others (system audio) separately, so speakers are labeled by source from the very first chunk.
  • Real speaker diarization. On the final pass, system audio is split into Speaker 1, Speaker 2… with per-speaker talk-time stats.
  • Live, not after-the-fact. Chunks are transcribed while you talk; a rolling summary and attention alerts appear during the call itself.
  • Plain-text output you own. Every meeting is a folder of markdown files under ~/Meetings/ — version it, grep it, script it.

Built for the whole meeting

From the first second of capture to a searchable, tagged, speaker-labeled transcript. A few highlights — see all features.

capture

Dual-channel recording

Mic (you) + system audio (others) captured in parallel, atomically handed off as 15s WAV chunks.

quality

Two-model pipeline

Fast small model live, higher-quality medium model on the final pass — with echo and duplicate filtering.

speakers

Diarization + talk time

System audio split into labeled speakers with a per-speaker duration & percentage footer.

live

Attention alerts

Get a macOS notification when someone says your name or a trigger word during the call.

live

Rolling summary

A resource-aware TextRank summary is written next to the transcript while you record.

tunable

Vocabulary & phrasebook

Hot-reloadable custom vocabulary biases the decoder; regex phrasebook rewrites transcripts on the fly.

import

Transcribe existing files

Point it at m4a/mp4/wav files — batch or one-off — for meetings recorded elsewhere.

reliable

Crash-safe by design

Atomic writes, an append-only transcript log, and stale-session detection mean no lost work on a hard kill.
